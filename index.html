Wireless AI Systems Lab

How is the role of communications changing in the AI era?

LLMs are now widely used, yet the fundamental role of communications remains largely unchanged. There is simply more data to transmit.

With Physical AI, communications take on a broader role.

  1. Agents need new ways to communicate. These methods can, in turn, be optimized by the agents themselves.
  2. Communication data can serve as a sensing modality, providing information about the environment that both Wireless AI and Physical AI need.

Our research focuses on two areas:

  • Physical-layer communications by and for agents
  • AI-driven 6G mobile networks

The Wireless AI Systems Lab (WAISL) is led by Prof. Hyung-Joo Moon.
